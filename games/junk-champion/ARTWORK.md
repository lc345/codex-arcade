# Junk Champion artwork

The arena and four cutout fighter rigs were generated specifically for this project
with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-29. No named game, franchise,
artist likeness or third-party character was requested. Generated artwork is
included for use and redistribution with this project; no exclusive copyright
claim is made for generated imagery. Original game code, rig animation, procedural
effects and sound synthesis are Apache-2.0. Godot retains its MIT license.

## Brief

- Arena: an empty lateral underground junkyard boxing ring, irregular black ink,
  worn painted surfaces, coral curtains, jade mat, clear character silhouette area.
- Fighters: a transparent 4 by 4 atlas. Each row contains torso/head, glove/forearm,
  boot/shin and impact. Rows are a taped yellow rookie, red boiler bruiser,
  turquoise fan boxer, and violet spring trickster. All face right in the atlas.
- The actual Godot rig independently positions limbs, leans the torso, stretches
  a punch, recoils, squashes, and changes footwork. These are not video clips or
  a mock screenshot covering the game.

The source PNGs are preserved as generated. Atlas regions are selected at runtime;
there is no destructive background-removal or asset repaint pipeline. Generated
asset edges and the assembled rigs must be inspected in actual gameplay.

Sources: OpenAI built-in image generation; https://godotengine.org/license/
