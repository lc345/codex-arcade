# Reel, Don't Break artwork

Created 2026-09-30 with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The inlet, fish and angler were newly generated for this project, not downloaded from a commercial game. The game draws the rod, reel, jetty, line and water feedback locally and animates the fish atlas in strips. The angler is a painted cutout with a load-driven lean, not a fully rigged character.

The final files are `inlet.png`, `fish-v2.png` and `angler.png`. Source outputs remain in the local image-generation archive. No source prompt includes user messages, Agent output, personal identities or third-party character references. No external service is used at runtime.

The project distributes these assets under the accompanying Apache-2.0 license. This is not a claim of exclusive rights in generated images. No external textures, fonts, recorded audio or franchise assets are included. `provenance.json` records file hashes and the hash of this prompt record. Audio is independently authored Web Audio synthesis in `../sound.js`.

## Background prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original background plate for an interactive fishing game called Reel, Don't Break. Create one high quality wide landscape 3:2 illustration, no text and no UI. Hand-painted gouache mixed with Japanese woodblock and mid-century illustrated nature book, visibly tactile paper and dry brush, NOT 3D, NOT vector flat art, NOT pixel art. A quiet rocky inlet shown as a continuous above-water / underwater cutaway. Water surface horizontal at exactly 28 percent of image height; top 28 percent: pale ivory daylight sky, coral-pink low clouds, distant sage green rocky hills with pine trees at right, tiny far lighthouse, horizon. Bottom 72 percent is a readable luminous turquoise to forest-teal underwater column. This water is translucent painted pigment with sparse handpainted caustic strokes, not dark opaque. Bottom corners have layered rounded painted rocks, ochre weeds, sea grass and a few coral-red leaves. Rich restrained teal, coral vermilion, chartreuse and ivory palette. Important gameplay empty space: the middle 70 percent width, between 30 and 83 percent of height, is open uncluttered water where moving fish and a line will be drawn by the game. Do not draw ANY fish, fisherman, people, boat, pier, fishing rod, line, bubbles or user interface; these are separate animated layers. No lettering, logo, border, caption, or watermark. This must feel like a beautiful authored indie game painted scene, with irregular painterly texture and bold graphic silhouettes, not stock scenery.

A kindly middle-aged woman with short dark hair under a broad mustard yellow rain hat, coral vermilion oilskin fishing jacket, dark forest green rolled trousers and yellow rubber boots. Broad confident patches of pigment, shadows woven with ochre hatching. Expressive kind face, not a stick figure, not geometric flat vector, not a toy 3D model, not photorealistic. ## Runtime treatment

- Background: aspect-preserving cover with its waterline aligned to the playfield.
- Atlas: measured row crops; small local strip displacements animate tails without editing the source PNG.
- Angler: anchored to the jetty, leaned by line tension; rod and reel are separate interactive shapes.
- Reduced motion: decorative tail flexing and ambient movement removed; gameplay distance, direction changes and warnings remain visible.
- All PNGs are copied into the project; no temporary or external image paths are runtime dependencies.
