# Sports Ten: Artwork

Ten original environment images made with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-10-02. Source PNGs are preserved; two backgrounds were revised with image_gen to remove static gameplay objects. All moving pieces, collisions and overlays are original Canvas drawings. Sound is synthesized locally with Web Audio, with no sampled commercial music or sounds.

Project distribution license: Apache-2.0. This does not assert exclusive copyright over AI-generated imagery. No commercial sports brand, player portrait, logo or game character is used. The sports are short solo arcade interpretations, not official sports simulations.
