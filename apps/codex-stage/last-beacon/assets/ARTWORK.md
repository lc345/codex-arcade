# Last Beacon artwork

Original survivor, guns and supply cases are built in Blender by tools/art/build-beacon-assets.py. No commercial game models, characters, maps or audio are copied. Runtime walk, recoil, heal and elimination poses animate the named local joints. Generated and scripted project assets are distributed under Apache-2.0 without a claim of exclusive copyright over AI-generated material.

The three UI icons (crosshair, heart-pulse, package-open) are from Lucide 0.468.0, https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/0.468.0/icons. Their ISC notice is LUCIDE-LICENSE. Other toolbar icons reuse the existing licensed Stage set. All icons are served locally. Sound effects are generated locally by sound.js with Web Audio; no sampled music or commercial game audio is used.
