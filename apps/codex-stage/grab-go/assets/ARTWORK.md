# Grab & Go artwork

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-10-02. Original cabinet and eight treasure designs; no commercial game art, audio, logos or characters were copied. These generated project assets are distributed under Apache-2.0 without a claim of exclusive copyright over AI-generated material.

- treasures.png source: exec-4a26b0b0-df89-4f0b-9217-6e838f9da8f3.png
- The PNGs are preserved unchanged. The renderer finds the alpha bounds of each of eight equal atlas cells, crops at draw time and animates their positions from the Matter.js simulation. Compact screens recompose the cabinet background from three local slices without stretching collectible sprites.
- The mechanical claw and moving shutter are original Canvas drawings. All sound is synthesized locally in sound.js with Web Audio; there are no sampled songs or commercial effects.
- Toolbar icons reuse the project's Lucide icons, whose ISC license is in ../assets/icons/LICENSE relative to the game directory.
